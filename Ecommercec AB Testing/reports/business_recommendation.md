# Business Recommendation: E-commerce A/B Test

## 1. Business Objective

The objective of this experiment is to evaluate whether a redesigned Buy Now button improves conversion rate, revenue per user, and contribution profit per user.

## 2. Key Findings

| Metric | Control | Treatment | Conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Conversion Rate | 8.80% | 9.67% | Significant improvement |
| Revenue per User | 81.52 | 86.72 | Improvement not statistically significant |
| Profit per User | 23.27 | 24.79 | Improvement not statistically significant |

The conversion rate increased by **0.872 percentage points**, equivalent to a **9.91% relative improvement**. This result remained statistically significant after Holm correction.

Revenue per user increased by 6.38%, while contribution profit per user increased by 6.50%. However, neither financial improvement was statistically significant after correction.

## 3. Financial Impact

The observed contribution profit difference was approximately **1.51 units per user**.

For 25,000 users, this corresponds to an estimated incremental profit of approximately **37,793 units**.

However, the approximate 95% confidence interval ranges from **-363 to 75,948 units**. Since this interval includes zero, the actual financial benefit remains uncertain.

## 4. Business Recommendation

**Decision: Continue testing before a full rollout.**

The redesigned button shows evidence of improving conversion, but the experiment does not establish a reliable improvement in profitability.

Before making a final decision, I recommend:

- Running a follow-up experiment with contribution profit per user as the primary metric.
- Calculating the required sample size based on a minimum commercially worthwhile profit improvement.
- Monitoring conversion, revenue, refunds, cancellations, and other relevant business metrics.
- Launching only when the results meet predefined statistical and business requirements.

## 5. Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic and does not represent real company data.
- Revenue and profit improvements were not statistically significant after multiple-testing correction.
- The results may not generalize to other customer populations or future traffic.
- The analysis does not establish why the treatment changes conversion or financial outcomes.

## 6. Final Conclusion

The experiment demonstrates that a higher conversion rate does not automatically guarantee higher profitability.

Although the redesigned button significantly improved conversion, its financial benefits remain uncertain. Therefore, further testing is needed before recommending a full rollout.
